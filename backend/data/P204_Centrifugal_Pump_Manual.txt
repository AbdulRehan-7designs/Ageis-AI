1. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
- Rated Flow Rate: 450 m³/h
- Design Pressure: 24.5 bar
- Operating Temperature: 70 °C - 85 °C (Alarm at 90 °C)
- Motor Power: 110 kW, 2950 RPM
- Bearing Type: Heavy Duty SKF 6314 Deep Groove Ball Bearing

2. VIBRATION LIMITS & DIAGNOSIS
- 0 - 4.5 mm/s: Acceptable condition.
- 4.5 - 7.1 mm/s: Elevated vibration. Likely causes: imbalance, slight coupling misalignment, loose foundation bolts.
- > 7.1 mm/s: Critical vibration limit. High risk of bearing race degradation, shaft deflection, or catastrophic mechanical seal failure.
