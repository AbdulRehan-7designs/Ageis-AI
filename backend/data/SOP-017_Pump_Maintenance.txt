STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: PUMP MAINTENANCE AND ISOLATION
SOP ID: SOP-017
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2024-01-15
CLASSIFICATION: CONFIDENTIAL
PLANT LOCATION: MRPL REFINERY UNIT 2

SECTION 1: SCOPE AND PURPOSE
This procedure defines standard maintenance protocols, vibration thresholds, isolation criteria, and safety checks for all centrifugal pumps across MRPL refinery process lines.

SECTION 2: VIBRATION THRESHOLDS FOR CENTRIFUGAL PUMPS (e.g., P-204, P-205, P-206)
- Normal Operating Range: 0.0 mm/s to 4.5 mm/s. Continuous operation permitted without intervention.
- Warning Threshold: 4.5 mm/s to 7.1 mm/s. Schedule routine inspection within 48 hours. Monitor bearing temperature closely.
- Critical Threshold: > 7.1 mm/s. Immediate action required. Isolate feed line and schedule emergency mechanical inspection.

SECTION 3: ISOLATION AND SHUTDOWN PROTOCOL (HITL REQUIREMENT)
- Shutdown of primary feed line pumps (including P-204) impacts Unit 2 throughput.
- MANDATORY: Mechanical isolation or pump shutdown MUST be authorized via Human-In-The-Loop (HITL) engineer approval.
- Step 1: Verify sensor readings and cross-check historical vibration trend.
- Step 2: Obtain engineer digital confirmation via AegisAI Workbench.
- Step 3: Close suction valve V-101 and discharge valve V-102. Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) applied.
- Step 4: Perform mechanical inspection of bearing assembly, coupling alignment, and impeller wear.
- Step 5: Replace or repair bearing assembly based on physical findings.

SECTION 4: COMPLIANCE & SAFETY CONSTRAINTS
- Maximum budget allocation for routine pump overhaul: Rs 75,000.
- All operational decisions must be logged to the sovereign audit database.
